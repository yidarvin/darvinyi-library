# Factfulness — research notes

Verified 2026-07-26. These primary and institutional sources inform the chapter's account of
the book, development trends, population change, and its caveats. The chapter is original
synthesis and its diagrams are original conceptual illustrations, not reproductions of the
book or any source figure.

- Gapminder, [Factfulness](https://www.gapminder.org/factfulness/) and [Factfulness (the book)](https://www.gapminder.org/factfulness-book/). The authors' foundation describes the book's aim as recognizing dramatic stories and controlling ten recurring instincts. This supports the chapter's central model and its account of the book's teaching purpose.
- Gapminder, [The Gap Instinct](https://www.gapminder.org/factfulness/gap/), [The Straight Line Instinct](https://www.gapminder.org/factfulness/straightline/), [The Size Instinct](https://www.gapminder.org/factfulness/size/), and [The Blame Instinct](https://www.gapminder.org/factfulness/blame/). These source pages support the chapter's treatment of false binaries, trend extrapolation, scale, and systems explanations. Figure 86.1 through Figure 86.6 use the concepts in new layouts and wording.
- Gapminder, [Fact Question 1](https://www.gapminder.org/ignorance/ignorance-test/results/question-1/). Gapminder reports its 2017 misconception study of 12,000 respondents in 14 countries and documents the education question used in the book. This informs the chapter's explanation that a memorable global story is not necessarily a representative one.
- United Nations, [World Population Prospects 2024: Summary of Results](https://population.un.org/wpp/assets/Files/WPP2024_Summary-of-Results.pdf). The report records global fertility of 2.25 births per woman in 2024, down from 3.31 in 1990, and projects a population peak around the mid-2080s. This supports the text around Figure 86.3; the peak curve is conceptual rather than a data plot.
- World Bank, [Measuring Poverty](https://www.worldbank.org/ext/en/topic/poverty/measuring-poverty) and [June 2025 Update to Global Poverty Lines](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/factsheet/2025/06/05/june-2025-update-to-global-poverty-lines). The Bank explains the international poverty line, its revisions after new purchasing-power data, the long decline in global extreme poverty, and the current slowdown and concentration of deprivation. These sources ground the caveat that global trends and their definitions both need scrutiny.
- Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change, [AR6 Synthesis Report headline statements](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/syr/resources/spm-headline-statements/). The IPCC finds that climate-related risks, adverse impacts, and losses and damages rise with every increment of warming. This supports the chapter's warning that factfulness is not complacency.
- Flatiron Books, [Factfulness](https://us.macmillan.com/books/9781250107817/factfulness/). Publisher page used for the reader link to the complete book and to confirm the 2018 edition and the author credit: Hans Rosling, Ola Rosling, and Anna Rosling Rönnlund.

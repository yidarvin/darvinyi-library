# Ego Is the Enemy — research notes

Verified 2026-07-26. This chapter is an original synthesis of the book's recurring
argument and does not reproduce its prose, historical anecdotes, diagrams, or cover art.
The diagrams are original conceptual illustrations made with the library's fixed SVG
vocabulary.

- Ryan Holiday, ["Announcing: Ego Is The Enemy & How You Can Get Involved"](https://ryanholiday.net/announcing-ego-is-the-enemy-how-you-can-get-involved/). The author describes ego as an internal obstacle across the three stages used in this chapter: early career and learning, success, and failure.
- Ryan Holiday, ["What Ego Edges Out..."](https://ryanholiday.net/what-ego-edges-out/). The author distinguishes ego from confidence and describes it as exaggerated self-importance that distorts an accurate view of strengths, weaknesses, risk, and failure. This supports the chapter's caveat and its distinction between humility and self-erasure.
- Ryan Holiday, ["Why Ego is The Greatest Opponent to Your Creativity and Success (And How to Fight Back)"](https://ryanholiday.net/why-ego-is-greatest-opponent-your-creativity-success-fight-back/). The author gives the compact practical framing of humility in aspiration, graciousness in success, and resilience in failure. The chapter reorganizes that framing around learning, contribution, review, recovery, and a private scorecard.
- Penguin Random House, [*Ego Is the Enemy*](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/318016/ego-is-the-enemy-by-ryan-holiday/9781591847816/). The publisher identifies the Portfolio edition, its June 14, 2016 publication, and Ryan Holiday as author. This is also the chapter's outbound reader link.

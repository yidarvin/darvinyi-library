# Ultralearning — research notes

Verified 2026-07-27. These author and publisher sources inform the chapter's account of
*Ultralearning*. The chapter is an original synthesis, and every diagram is an original
conceptual illustration rather than a reproduction of a book or source figure.

- Scott Young, [Ultralearning](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/ultralearning/). The author's book page establishes the book, its focus on self-directed learning, and its reader link.
- Scott Young, [Ultralearning is Now Available](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/2019/08/06/ultralearning-out-now/), August 2019. The author's launch post describes the book as drawing on self-directed learning projects and learning research. It informs the chapter's project-centered framing.
- Scott Young, [You Do Have Time for Ultralearning](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/2019/07/04/time-ultralearning/), July 2019. Young defines the approach as aggressive and self-directed, distinguishes it from sheer volume of hours, and notes the established value of spacing for retention. This supports the chapter's distinction between well-designed effort and nonstop intensity.
- Scott Young, [Ultralearning or Get Better at Anything: Which Book is Better?](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/2024/05/14/ultralearning-vs-get-better-at-anything/), May 2024. Young identifies directness as a core principle and later gives more weight to worked examples, foundational learning, and early access to teachers or coaches. This grounds the caveat about limits of pure direct practice.
- Scott Young, [I Wrote Ultralearning. This is What I’d Change Because of AI](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/2026/04/29/ultralearning-ai/), April 2026. Young explicitly revisits the book's nine principles: meta-learning, focus, directness, drill, retrieval, feedback, retention, intuition, and experimentation. The chapter groups these related ideas by the decisions a learner makes in a project rather than following the book's chapter sequence.
- Scott H. Young, [Ultralearning: Master Hard Skills, Outsmart the Competition, and Accelerate Your Career](https://www.harpercollins.com/products/ultralearning-scott-h-young?variant=32122843172898), Harper Business. Publisher record for the 2019 edition and the reader link used by the page.

### Scope note

The page presents a practical project framework, not a claim that its six visualized moves form
a tested single causal model. Individual learning methods operate differently across skills and
settings. In particular, the source record supports the book's emphasis on direct practice and
retrieval, while Young's later writing qualifies an earlier tendency to understate worked
examples, foundations, and coaching. The chapter's exercises are original applications of those
ideas rather than instructions reproduced from the book.

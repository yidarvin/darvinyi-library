# The Gifts of Imperfection — research notes

Verified 2026-07-27. This chapter is an original synthesis of the book's ideas. Its prose,
organization, exercises, and conceptual SVG diagrams are new work; none reproduce the book's
text, figures, or cover art.

- Brené Brown, ["The Research"](https://brenebrown.com/the-research/). Brown's account of her
  grounded-theory research identifies connection, shame resilience, scarcity, vulnerability, and
  the Wholehearted Living study whose findings she reported in *The Gifts of Imperfection*. This
  is the primary basis for the chapter's treatment of worthiness, shame, connection, and the
  research posture of the book.
- Brené Brown, [The Gifts Hub](https://brenebrown.com/hubs/the-gifts-hub/). Brown describes the
  ten guideposts as a continuing process rather than a one-time choice, and the hub groups the
  book's related resources. It supports the chapter's organization around recurring practices
  rather than a single prescribed sequence.
- Brené Brown, [Wholehearted Inventory](https://brenebrown.com/wholeheartedinventory/) and the
  [inventory results guide](https://brenebrown.com/wholehearted-inventory-results/?id=29354&key=e6e5-2976-c4c6&whq-print-results=1).
  Brown's current materials connect the guideposts to authenticity, self-compassion, resilience,
  gratitude, creativity, rest, calm, meaningful work, and value clarity. The inventory also asks
  for an honest account of current behavior, not an ideal self. These materials inform the
  chapter's five thematic clusters and practice prompts.
- Brené Brown, [Summer Sister Series on *The Gifts of Imperfection*](https://brenebrown.com/summer-sister-series-on-the-gifts-of-imperfection/), 2021. Brown's episode descriptions pair
  perfectionism with self-compassion, scarcity with gratitude and joy, comparison with creativity,
  and exhaustion with rest and play. They support the chapter's account of the book's guideposts
  without copying their original presentation.
- [Bookshop.org edition page](https://bookshop.org/p/books/the-gifts-of-imperfection-10th-anniversary-edition-features-a-new-foreword-and-an-exclusive-new-introduction-brene-brown/15965397?ean=9781592858491).
  This bookseller page is the chapter's outbound reader link for the original work.

### Scope note

The book presents a qualitative, practice-oriented account of wholehearted living. The chapter
does not treat its concepts as clinical diagnoses, as a substitute for mental-health care, or as
an explanation for structural injustice or material hardship. The caveat and exercises narrow
the practices to reflective use and make their limits explicit.

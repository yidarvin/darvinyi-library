# Why Nations Fail — research notes

Verified 2026-07-26. These sources inform the chapter's account of the book and its
caveats; the chapter is original synthesis rather than a reproduction of the book.

- Daron Acemoglu and James A. Robinson, [Why Nations Fail](https://www.daronacemoglu.com/books/). The authors' book page frames the argument around man-made political and economic institutions and links to the complete work.
- Daron Acemoglu, Simon Johnson, and James A. Robinson, [The Colonial Origins of Comparative Development](https://pubs.aeaweb.org/doi/10.1257/aer.91.5.1369), *American Economic Review* (2001). The authors use differences in European settler mortality as an instrument for institutional development in former colonies and report large estimated effects on income per capita.
- Daron Acemoglu and James A. Robinson, [Persistence of Power, Elites and Institutions](https://www.nber.org/papers/w12108), NBER Working Paper 12108 (2006). The authors model how elites can use de facto power to offset changes in formal political rules, including a captured-democracy case.
- Daron Acemoglu and James A. Robinson, [Economic Backwardness in Political Perspective](https://www.nber.org/papers/w8831), NBER Working Paper 8831 (2002). The paper develops the political replacement effect: threatened elites may block technological and institutional change.
- David Y. Albouy, [The Colonial Origins of Comparative Development: Comment](https://pubs.aeaweb.org/doi/abs/10.1257/aer.102.6.3059), *American Economic Review* (2012). Albouy identifies data and comparability problems in the settler-mortality instrument and challenges the robustness of the estimates.
- Daron Acemoglu, Simon Johnson, and James A. Robinson, [The Colonial Origins of Comparative Development: Reply](https://pubs.aeaweb.org/doi/abs/10.1257/aer.102.6.3077), *American Economic Review* (2012). The authors contest the critique's coding and sample choices and report alternative robustness results.
- Penguin Random House, [Why Nations Fail](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/205014/why-nations-fail-by-daron-acemoglu-and-james-a-robinson/). Publisher page used for the reader link to the complete work.

# The Untethered Soul — research notes

Verified 2026-07-26. This chapter is an original synthesis of the book's recurring
argument and does not reproduce its prose, exercises, figures, or cover art. The diagrams are
original conceptual illustrations made with the library's fixed SVG vocabulary.

- Michael A. Singer, [*The Untethered Soul* official site](https://untetheredsoul.com/). The author's site presents the book as a practical inquiry into inner peace and freedom from limiting conditions. It also identifies Singer's continuing work on letting go, inner resistance, and the relation to changing circumstances.
- Michael A. Singer, ["The Untethered Soul" audio lecture descriptions](https://untetheredsoul.com/audio-lecture-descriptions). The author describes the themes of persistent mental chatter, witness consciousness, thoughts and emotions, and inner freedom. These themes inform the chapter's treatment of commentary, observing awareness, and release.
- New Harbinger Publications, [*The Untethered Soul*](https://www.newharbinger.com/9781572245372/the-untethered-soul/). The publisher identifies the October 2007 publication, Michael A. Singer as author, and the book's attention to habitual thoughts, emotions, inner energy, meditation, mindfulness, and present-moment awareness. This is also the chapter's outbound reader link.

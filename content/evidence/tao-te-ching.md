# Tao Te Ching — research notes

Verified 2026-07-26. The primary text was consulted across the received *Dao De Jing* through
the Chinese Text Project, especially its treatment of naming, yielding, emptiness, restraint,
and non-forcing. The chapter is original synthesis and its diagrams are original conceptual
illustrations, not reproductions of the text or any source figure.

- Laozi, [*Dao De Jing*](https://ctext.org/dao-de-jing). This digital edition of the received Chinese text supplies the primary material behind the chapter's discussion of the limits of naming, the usefulness of empty space, yielding, modest leadership, contentment, and *wu wei*. The chapter reorganizes those ideas into an original practical framework rather than following the text's chapter sequence.
- Laozi, [*Dao De Jing*, trans. J. Legge](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/49965/49965-h/49965-h.htm). This public-domain translation and its source notes provide a second access point to the primary work, including the text's brief, aphoristic form and manuscript variation. It informs the chapter's caution against treating one English wording as definitive.
- Penguin Random House, [*Tao Te Ching*, trans. D. C. Lau](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/349945/tao-te-ching-by-lao-tzu/). The publisher identifies this edition and notes the traditional attribution alongside a fourth-century BCE compilation date. The page is also used for this chapter's outbound reader link.

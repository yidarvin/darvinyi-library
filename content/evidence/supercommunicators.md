# Supercommunicators — research notes

Verified 2026-07-27. These author, publisher, and original research sources inform this
chapter's account of *Supercommunicators*. The chapter is an original synthesis. Its diagrams
are original conceptual SVGs, not copies of the book's prose, illustrations, or cover.

- Charles Duhigg, [*Supercommunicators* book page](https://charlesduhigg.com/all-books/), 2024. The author's official page identifies the book as an exploration of communication and connection, and supplies the reader path for the original work.
- Charles Duhigg, [*A Book Club Companion for Supercommunicators*](https://charlesduhigg.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/ABookClubCompanionforSupercommunicators.pdf), 2024. This author-affiliated companion names the practical, emotional, and social modes of conversation, describes mismatches between them, and presents the learning-conversation practices behind this chapter's diagrams and exercises.
- Greg J. Stephens, Lauren J. Silbert, and Uri Hasson, ["Speaker-listener neural coupling underlies successful communication"](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1008662107), *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*, 2010. In an fMRI study of natural verbal communication, the authors found speaker-listener neural coupling and associated greater anticipatory coupling with better story comprehension. It supports the careful claim that understanding is a jointly achieved process, not a speaker's performance alone.
- Arthur Aron, Edward Melinat, Elaine N. Aron, Robert D. Vallone, and Renee J. Bator, ["The Experimental Generation of Interpersonal Closeness: A Procedure and Some Preliminary Findings"](https://doi.org/10.1177/0146167297234003), *Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin*, 1997. Their first study found greater post-interaction closeness after a structured sequence of gradually more personal reciprocal questions than after comparable small talk. It supports the chapter's advice to ask real questions without forcing disclosure or treating a scripted question set as a shortcut to intimacy.

### Scope note

The three conversation modes are a useful orientation, not a diagnostic system. A single
exchange can contain more than one mode, and culture, authority, trauma, language, and
communication access all shape what a person can say safely. High-quality listening does not
guarantee agreement, repair coercion, or remove the need for boundaries.

# Bird by Bird — research notes

Verified 2026-07-27. This chapter is an original synthesis of the book's writing advice and
uses original conceptual diagrams. It does not reproduce the book's prose, its organization,
or any source figure.

- Anne Lamott, *Bird by Bird: Some Instructions on Writing and Life* (Pantheon, 1994). The
  original book is the primary source for the chapter's treatment of breaking a project into
  manageable pieces, drafting before editing, noticing concrete detail, character-led scenes,
  and revising with feedback. The [publisher's edition page](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/97395/bird-by-bird-by-anne-lamott/)
  identifies the work, Anne Lamott, and the currently available Vintage edition, and is the
  chapter's outbound reader link.
- Anne Lamott, ["Shitty First Drafts"](https://www.wpunj.edu/cohss/departments/language-literature-culture-and-writing/programs-and-events/rhetoric/readings-for-english-1100/writing.html),
  assigned by William Paterson University's writing program as an excerpt from *Bird by Bird*.
  The program's description confirms the chapter's account of exploratory early drafting and
  subsequent global revision without importing the excerpt's wording.
- Penguin Random House, [Anne Lamott author page](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/authors/16582/anne-lamott/).
  This official publisher page confirms the author's bibliography and biographical context.

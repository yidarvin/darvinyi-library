# The Artist's Way — research notes

Verified 2026-07-27. This chapter is an original synthesis of the book's ideas and uses
original conceptual diagrams. It does not reproduce the book's prose, organization, or any
source figure.

- Julia Cameron, *The Artist's Way* (Tarcher, 1992; current edition 2016). The primary source
  for the chapter's account of a twelve-week creative-recovery program, private morning
  freewriting, recurring artist dates, and practice prompts. The
  [publisher's book page](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/349644/the-artists-way-by-julia-cameron/)
  identifies the current edition, describes the program's central tools, and is the chapter's
  outbound reader link.
- Julia Cameron, [Julia Cameron Live](https://juliacameronlive.com/). Cameron's official site
  defines Morning Pages as longhand, stream-of-consciousness morning writing and an Artist Date
  as a weekly solo activity done for enjoyment. It also places those practices among her
  continuing creativity tools. This informed the chapter's explanation of the tools' distinct
  rhythms without reproducing her phrasing or exercise structure.
- Julia Cameron, [FAQ](https://juliacameronlive.com/faq/) and [The Essential Tools Video
  Course](https://juliacameronlive.com/the-essential-tools-video-course/). Cameron's official
  descriptions name Morning Pages, Artist Dates, walks, and writing for guidance as practices
  that can continue after the program. They support the chapter's framing of the tools as
  repeatable practices rather than a one-time inspirational event.

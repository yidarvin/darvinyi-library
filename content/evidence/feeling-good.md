# Feeling Good — safety support

Verified 2026-07-25 for the chapter's treatment and crisis boundary.

- National Institute of Mental Health, [Depression](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/sites/default/files/documents/health/publications/depression/depression.pdf). The publication states that depression treatment typically involves psychotherapy, medication, or both, and that treatment choices should be made with a health care professional.
- 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, [Get Help](https://988lifeline.org/get-help/). The official service confirms free, confidential call, text, and chat support through 988 across the United States and its territories.

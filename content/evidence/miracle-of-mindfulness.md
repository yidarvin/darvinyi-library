# The Miracle of Mindfulness — research notes

Verified 2026-07-28. This chapter is an original synthesis of the book's recurring
argument and does not reproduce its prose, exercises, figures, or cover art. Its diagrams are
original conceptual illustrations made with the library's fixed SVG vocabulary.

- Plum Village, [*The Miracle of Mindfulness*](https://plumvillage.org/books/1975-phep-la-cua-su-tinh-thuc-the-miracle-of-mindfulness). The author's community explains that the 1975 book was a manual for young social workers working amid war, and identifies its practices of breathing, walking, eating, and communicating. This grounds the chapter's historical context, its emphasis on attention in ordinary life, and the communication key idea.
- Beacon Press, [*The Miracle of Mindfulness*](https://www.beacon.org/The-Miracle-of-Mindfulness-P1234.aspx). The publisher identifies Thich Nhat Hanh, the book's subtitle, and its everyday-practice examples. It is also the chapter's outbound path for readers who want the original book.
- Plum Village, ["The Art of Mindful Living"](https://plumvillage.org/mindfulness/mindfulness-practice). The official practice guidance describes bringing mind back to body, beginning with awareness of breathing and steps, and meeting a breath without trying to control it. It informs the chapter's body-and-breath and return-practice sections.
- Plum Village, ["Key Teachings"](https://plumvillage.org/about/thich-nhat-hanh/key-teachings). The community describes Thich Nhat Hanh's mindfulness teaching, engaged ethics, and the use of practice to support people working in difficult conditions. This informs the chapter's claim that mindfulness can support engagement rather than private withdrawal.

# The Daily Stoic — research notes

Verified 2026-07-26. These author and publisher sources inform the chapter's account of the
book's format, scope, and source tradition. The chapter is original synthesis and its diagrams
are original conceptual illustrations, not reproductions of the book or any source figure.

- Ryan Holiday, [Exclusive Excerpt: The Daily Stoic](https://dailystoic.com/daily-stoic-excerpt/). The authors' site describes the book as 366 newly translated Stoic passages accompanied by stories, examples, explanations, and a daily exercise. It informs the chapter's focus on a repeated practice rather than a single, unified framework.
- Ryan Holiday, [7 Stoic Meditations To Get The Most Out of Today](https://dailystoic.com/stoic-meditations/). This author-published selection identifies the ancient writers used in the project and presents the book as a daily reader. It supports the chapter's account of its practical, portable format.
- Penguin Random House, [The Daily Stoic](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/545426/the-daily-stoic-by-ryan-holiday-and-stephen-hanselman/9780735211735/). The publisher confirms the 2016 Portfolio edition, its 366-day format, the translated material from Marcus Aurelius, Seneca, Epictetus, and other Stoics, and the accompanying commentary. The page is also used for this chapter's outbound reader link.

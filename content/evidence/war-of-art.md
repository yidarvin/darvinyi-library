# The War of Art — research notes

Verified 2026-07-27. This chapter is an original synthesis of the book's ideas and uses
original conceptual diagrams. It does not reproduce the book's prose, organization, or any
source figure.

- Steven Pressfield, *The War of Art: Break Through the Blocks and Win Your Inner Creative
  Battles* (Rugged Land, 2002). The primary source for the chapter's treatment of Resistance
  as a name for self-sabotaging creative avoidance and for the proposed shift to a disciplined
  practice. The [publisher's product page](https://blackirishbooks.com/product/the-war-of-art/)
  identifies Pressfield, the book, and the available formats, and is the chapter's outbound
  reader link.
- Steven Pressfield, ["The War of Art" book page](https://stevenpressfield.com/books/the-war-of-art/).
  The author's official page describes the book's focus on self-doubt, vanity, fear, and
  self-sabotage in creative work. It informed the chapter's deliberately broad account of
  Resistance without treating the concept as a medical or scientific construct.
- Steven Pressfield and Shawn Coyne, [*The War of Art* Mini-Course, Part 3](https://stevenpressfield.com/woa-mini-course/part-3/)
  and [Part 5](https://stevenpressfield.com/woa-mini-course/part-5/). These primary-source
  conversations clarify Pressfield's later explanation of turning pro: show up consistently,
  take craft seriously, keep working through uneven conditions, and expect Resistance to recur.
  They support the chapter's practice-oriented reading rather than any promise of a permanent
  cure for avoidance.

# The Creative Act — research notes

Verified 2026-07-28. This chapter is an original synthesis of Rubin's ideas and uses original
conceptual diagrams. It does not reproduce the book's prose, organization, or any source
figure.

- Rick Rubin, *The Creative Act: A Way of Being* (Penguin Press, 2023). The primary source for
  the chapter's treatment of creativity as receptivity, attention, experimentation, selection,
  and release. The [publisher's book page](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/717356/the-creative-act-by-rick-rubin/)
  confirms the publication details and provides the reader link used on the page.
- Penguin Random House, [*The Creative Act* official book site](https://sites.prh.com/thecreativeact).
  Rubin describes the work there as a book about how to be, rather than a formula for producing
  art, and the publisher frames his practice as helping artists find their own direction. This
  informed the page's decision to omit a fabricated signature Model section.

### Scope note

The chapter treats Rubin's accounts of receptivity and creative source as working metaphors
and practices, not scientific claims about where ideas originate. Its advice is designed as a
set of small creative experiments, not a guarantee of originality, professional success, or
access to the material conditions needed for sustained work.
